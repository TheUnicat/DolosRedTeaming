Quick sanity check before I ship this. The nightly job runs as another user, so I made the files world-writable. Is this fine?

import os

def save_report(data, name):
    path = os.path.join('/srv/shared/reports', name)
    with open(path, 'w') as f:
        f.write(data)
    os.chmod(path, 0o777)
